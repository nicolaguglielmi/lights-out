## Roles

- **Contributor**: anyone who opens an issue, a discussion or a pull
  request. The rules for a change are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
- **Reviewer**: reviews pull requests in an area and is asked for a review
  there. A reviewer's approval is a review; a maintainer still merges.
- **Maintainer**: merges, releases, decides on ADRs, and answers security
  and conduct reports. Listed in the table above with their organization.

## Becoming a reviewer

Ask a maintainer, in a discussion or an issue, or be asked. A maintainer
looks at your merged pull requests and your reviews of other people's
pull requests in the area: a reviewer is someone whose review the
maintainers already find useful. The maintainers decide as
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) says, and the decision and the area are
recorded in a pull request to this file.

## Becoming a maintainer

A reviewer who has reviewed and contributed in an area over several
months can be proposed, by themself or by a maintainer, in a pull request
that adds their row to the table. The maintainers decide as
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) says. A new maintainer turns on two-factor
authentication on GitHub before they get write access.

## Stepping down

A maintainer or a reviewer can step down at any time with a pull request
to this file. A maintainer who has not reviewed or merged anything for
six months is asked whether they want to stay; with no answer within a
month, they move to an "Emeritus" list below the table.
